Nexus AI Business OS

Your AI-powered business operating system.

Nexus AI Business OS is an intelligent platform designed to help entrepreneurs and small businesses manage, automate, and grow their operations through AI-powered assistance.

Overview

Nexus brings business management, artificial intelligence, persistent memory, and automation together in one platform.

Core Features

- AI-powered business assistant
- Multiple specialized AI departments
- Persistent business memory
- Business planning and strategy
- Intelligent workflow automation
- AI-powered research and assistance
- Google Gemini integration
- OpenAI integration
- Supabase backend and database

Technology Stack

- Frontend: React / TypeScript
- Backend: Supabase
- Database: PostgreSQL
- AI: Google Gemini and OpenAI
- Hosting: External deployment support

Technology components are subject to the current implementation.

Project Vision

Our mission is to make intelligent business tools more accessible by creating an AI operating system that helps individuals and organizations work smarter, automate tasks, and build sustainable businesses.

Development Status

Nexus AI Business OS is actively under development. Features, integrations, and capabilities are continuously being improved.
